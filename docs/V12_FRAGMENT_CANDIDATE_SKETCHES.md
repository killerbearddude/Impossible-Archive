# v1.2 Fragment Candidate Sketches

**Status:** v27.3 non-runtime design fixture  
**Scope:** content planning only  
**Runtime effect:** none

These sketches are deliberately not represented in source models, loaders, parsers, or CLI commands. They are examples of content units that could eventually inform a v1.2 fragment-composition design after v1.1 runtime stability is complete.

## Sketch format

Each sketch records: purpose, likely tags, suggested spec fields, compatibility notes, and risks. Field names are descriptive prose, not a schema.

## Candidate sketches

### 1. Canal Toll Bureau Fragment

- Likely tags: `water_access`, `bureaucratic`, `ledger_heavy`, `artifact_rich`
- Purpose: adds water-control offices, canal toll evidence, and lock/sluice conflict pressure.
- Suggested fields: institution archetypes, economic pressures, recordkeeping styles, artifact media.
- Compatibility notes: pairs well with delta, wetland, arid basin, and urban specs.
- Risks: can become too close to the fixed historical fixture if titles and artifacts are not localized.

### 2. Seasonal Camp Oath Fragment

- Likely tags: `nomadic`, `mobile_polity`, `oral_archive`, `oath_culture`
- Purpose: models portable authority, oath witnesses, and seasonal memory disputes.
- Suggested fields: social actor archetypes, ritual pressures, recordkeeping styles, evidence distortion modes.
- Compatibility notes: strongest with steppe, tundra, and pastoral specs.
- Risks: can conflict with heavy static bureaucracy unless mediated by market hosts or seasonal judges.

### 3. Harbor Salvage Court Fragment

- Compatibility notes: works with deltas, islands, lagoons, reefs, and drowned-causeway landscapes.
- Risks: salvage evidence can overproduce fragments unless public discovery is capped.
